Customer: Zenith Formulations Ltd.
Product Name: Atorvastatin Calcium Tablets 20mg
Batch Number: ATV-FDF-0817
Complaint Date: 12 July 2026

Description:
During routine dissolution testing at our site, tablets from the above batch showed
dissolution results of 68% at 30 minutes against a specification of not less than 80%.
Three additional tablets were tested with similar out-of-specification results. No
visual defects were observed. This appears to be a formulation or manufacturing process
deviation rather than a safety issue, but it does affect product efficacy and GMP
compliance, so we are logging it as a quality complaint pending your investigation.

Submitted by: R. Fernandes, QC Supervisor
